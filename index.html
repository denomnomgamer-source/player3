About Glowwave FM

Glowwave FM is a free internet radio station from the Glow Network. It streams live around the clock, works in any modern browser on phone, tablet and desktop, and needs no sign-up or app. Press play to listen live, watch the audio visualizer, add bass boost, set a sleep timer and keep up with world headlines while you listen.

Looking for more? Try the GlowWave Player, the relaxed GlowWave Chill stream or GlowTV Radio.

Made for listening

Live audio visualizer

Switch between bars, a waveform line and a glowing ring, drawn from the live audio. Go fullscreen for a screensaver.

Tab icon that moves

While you listen, the browser tab icon becomes a tiny live visualizer.

Bass boost and themes

Add low end with the bass slider and pick a colour theme. Your settings are remembered.

Reliable stream

Keyboard shortcuts

Space plays or pauses, M mutes, V changes style, F goes fullscreen, and the arrow keys change volume.

Questions about Glowwave FM

How do I listen to GlowWave FM?

Press the play button on this page. The station streams live in your browser on desktop and mobile.

Is Glowwave FM free?

Yes. Glowwave FM is free to listen to, with no sign-up and no app to install.

Does Glowwave FM broadcast 24/7?

Yes. The station streams live around the clock, and the player switches to a backup stream if the main one drops.

Why is there no sound after pressing play?

Check your device volume and the volume slider. Browsers only start audio after you press play.

Where do the news headlines come from?

The ticker links to Reuters and Bellingcat coverage of world politics and conflict. Each headline opens the original article.
